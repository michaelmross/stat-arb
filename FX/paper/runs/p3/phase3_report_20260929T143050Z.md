This is the decomposition the spec asks for in §3: separating the venue's price grid and the fact that three pairs do not tick at the same instant from anything that could be a real cross-market lag. The bottom row is an upper bound on the last of those, not a measurement of it — the τ→0 intercept still contains whatever asynchrony survives at τ→0, since quote ages are bounded by τ but never zero. ## 7. Standing conclusion

The deterministic triangle is closed on this feed: 0 cost-exceeding cycles in 133 hours of synchronized quotes. H1 holds, as the interdealer literature has implied since the late 2000s -- at a 2.09 bp three-leg cost and retail latency, there is nothing to take. The residual has σ = 0.098 pip against a 1.30 pip round trip. Of that, 9% is the quote grid and 6% is cross-pair asynchrony, leaving at most 0.090 pip that could be independent pricing of the cross. No live trades were taken, which is the correct outcome when the gate fails: the reversion available inside 30 seconds does not pay for one EUR/GBP spread, so there is no threshold worth trading. The deliverable is the census, and the census is negative in the same direction as the ETF-pairs and futures-spread work: the structure is real, measurable, and smaller than the cost of touching it.
